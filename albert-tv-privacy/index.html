Albert TV Privacy Policy

Albert TV is a private app for one household. It shows our Nest thermostat and front door camera on our own TV, and lets us change the temperature.

What it uses: thermostat readings and settings, the live doorbell video, and the home and room names Google provides.

What it does with that: it shows them on our TV. It doesn't record or store the video, doesn't sell or share any of this information, and doesn't use it for advertising. Sign-in credentials are stored on our own computer.

Removing access: access can be removed at any time in Google's Partner Connections settings or your Google Account.
